# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At Material, we believe in creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to the project.

We are committed to making collaboration a positive experience, regardless of experience level, background, or technical expertise.

## Our Standards

We encourage all contributors to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different perspectives.
- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Focus on improving the project together.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Help newcomers feel welcome.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive or inappropriate language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer through GitHub.

Reports will be reviewed with care and handled as respectfully as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate action. Depending on the situation, this may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, or restriction from project participation.

## Our Goal

Let's build Material through collaboration, respect, creativity, and shared learning.

**Be respectful. Build responsibly. Grow together.**
