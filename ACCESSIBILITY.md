# Accessibility

Accessibility is an important part of building Material. We want everyone to be able to browse products, explore product details, read reviews, and use the shopping experience comfortably, regardless of their abilities or the technologies they use.

This document explains our accessibility priorities, contributor expectations, and how to report accessibility barriers.

## Priorities

Material aims to provide an inclusive and user-friendly shopping experience.

Our accessibility priorities include:

- **Keyboard navigation:** Make important website features accessible without requiring a mouse.
- **Readable content:** Use clear language, readable typography, and appropriate text contrast.
- **Visual accessibility:** Avoid relying only on colors to communicate important information.
- **Screen reader support:** Provide meaningful labels and descriptions for interactive elements and product images.
- **Responsive experience:** Make the storefront usable across different screen sizes and devices.
- **Simple navigation:** Keep menus, buttons, product sections, and shopping interactions understandable.

These are ongoing goals, not a claim of verified accessibility conformance.

## Contributor expectations

Contributors are encouraged to consider accessibility whenever they introduce or modify user-facing features.

Please:

- Use semantic HTML elements wherever appropriate.
- Provide accessible names for buttons, links, and form fields.
- Add meaningful alternative text to informative images.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus indicators.
- Ensure text remains readable against its background.
- Avoid unnecessary animations and provide appropriate alternatives where possible.
- Test important interactions using keyboard navigation.

Include relevant accessibility considerations when submitting changes that affect the user interface.

## Reporting accessibility issues

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using Material, please report it through the repository's GitHub Issues section.

[Report an issue](https://github.com/BlockNotes-4515/material-commerce/issues)

When reporting an issue, useful details include:

- The page or feature where the problem occurred.
- What you were trying to accomplish.
- What happened and what you expected.
- Browser and operating system information.
- Any assistive technology used, if relevant.

Screenshots or recordings are welcome but optional. You do not need to disclose any personal or medical information.

### Severity

We consider accessibility issues based on their impact:

- **Critical:** A major shopping feature cannot be accessed or completed.
- **High:** An important interaction is difficult or impossible for some users.
- **Medium:** A feature is usable but creates unnecessary difficulty.
- **Low:** A minor usability or accessibility improvement.

Maintainers may review and assign the appropriate severity.

### How we respond

We aim to:

- Review reported accessibility issues.
- Ask for additional information when needed.
- Share progress through the relevant GitHub issue.
- Consider practical workarounds where available.
- Verify improvements before closing reported issues.

Response and resolution times may vary depending on the issue and available maintenance capacity.
